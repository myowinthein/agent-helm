**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## Review output and actions

Agent Helm provides workflows and optional local helpers for your coding agent. The agent's output can be incorrect, incomplete or outdated, including code, tests, configuration, documentation and legal drafts. Review proposed changes and verify results in the relevant project and environment before relying on them. A generated test or an agent's statement that a check passed does not by itself show that the check ran or protects the intended behavior.

Depending on the workflow, tool access and how you run it, the agent can modify files or configuration, run commands, rewrite Git history or publish work. Review the scope, targets and effects before running workflows or granting permissions. Use appropriate recovery measures where work or data could be lost.

Agent Helm's Markdown instructions guide the agent. They do not themselves enforce tool permissions or prevent mistakes.

## Legal drafts and external resources

The legal workflow assists with drafting documents from project evidence and confirmed facts. Its output is not a substitute for qualified legal advice. A generated document or approval of its content does not establish compliance with applicable law.

The tools, services and external resources used with Agent Helm can change. Review their current documentation and terms when relevant to your work. Links do not guarantee an external resource's accuracy or suitability.
